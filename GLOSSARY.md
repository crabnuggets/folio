# Folio glossary

## Monthly sheet language

**Monthly sheet**:
Named incoming and outgoing amounts assigned to one calendar month.

**Section**:
A heading organizing related items on a monthly sheet, with a subtotal calculated from those items.
_Avoid_: Group.

**Item**:
A named thing tracked with a monthly amount, such as salary, house savings, or a credit card bill.
_Avoid_: Record, transaction, account as a general name for every item.

**Monthly amount**:
The amount assigned to an item for a reporting month. For a credit card, this is the bill amount being accounted for that month, not the amount paid.

**Monthly remainder**:
The monthly sheet's entered incoming amounts minus its entered outgoing amounts, including savings and investment contributions. It is not a bank balance or a measure of consumption.

## Financial terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting month | Calendar month to which an item contributes in the monthly plan; distinct from its actual payment date. |
| Monthly surplus | Recorded income minus recognized spending minus completed savings/investment allocations. Not a bank balance. |
| Projected surplus | Expected full-month income minus expected full-month spending and allocations, with actuals replacing matched estimates. |
| Liquid balance | Latest observed balance of accounts explicitly included in spendable cash, with observation timestamps. |
| Account | A bank, cash, credit card, investment, or savings account; may be tracked only at summary level. |
| Statement | A credit card billing cycle, its new spending and adjustments, total amount due, and due date. |
| Statement spending | New cycle charges plus fees/interest minus cycle refunds; excludes prior balances and payments. |
| Obligation | Amount still due for a bill. It affects cash planning, not a second expense. |
| Payment | Movement of cash settling an obligation; not additional spending when the statement has already been recognized. |
| Allocation | Completed transfer of funds from spendable cash to savings or investments; reduces monthly surplus but is not consumption. |
| Internal transfer | Movement between owned accounts; no income or spending. A transfer may have an allocation purpose. |
| Draft | Proposed record requiring validation and, initially, user confirmation before it affects totals. |
| Posting | Confirmed financial record that participates in deterministic summaries. |
| Recurrence | Template generating expected items, never evidence that income arrived or a bill was paid. |
| Evidence | Source metadata supporting a draft, such as an email message ID or a statement page reference. |
| Balance snapshot | User-entered or imported account balance at a stated time. Not reconstructed from incomplete transaction tracking. |
| Coverage | Which expected income, bills, and allocations have been confirmed for a month. |
| Reconciliation | Linking independent observations of the same financial event without counting it twice. |

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Funding boundary | User-designated main funding accounts; distinct from all owned or liquid accounts. |
| Monthly funding remainder | Confirmed inflows minus outflows crossing the funding boundary during the reporting month, excluding opening cash. |
| Personal spending envelope | Separate owned spending account funded by transfers; funding is not consumption. |
| Envelope return | Unused money transferred back from the spending account; not salary. |
| Claim | Expected reimbursement, which may be pending or partially received. |
| Recovery | Confirmed reimbursement attributed to a recognized cost, reducing net personal cost once. |
| Legacy monthly review balance | Original spreadsheet formula result retained for comparison, not posted as cash or spending. |
